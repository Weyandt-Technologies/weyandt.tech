## How I work

You work with me, not an account team. I don't resell hardware, software, or managed IT, so if I tell you something is worth buying, that's the only reason. If a problem is outside what I do well I'll tell you.

Most engagements are [fractional CISO retainers](/fractional-ciso/) or [assessments](/services/infosec/). Some are [engineering work](/services/software/). All of them start with a conversation.
