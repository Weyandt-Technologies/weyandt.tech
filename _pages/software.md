I spent several years at Amazon on the software behind EC2's Internet Gateway, which moves a large share of the traffic in and out of AWS. A system like that has to be secure from the start, easy to see into, and boring to run.

Smaller systems need the same things. The cost of getting it wrong is just as high and there's less money to fix it afterward.

{% include feature_row %}

## Security is a design decision

Most of the expensive security problems I get asked to look at were decided early. How login works, where the data is kept, what one part of the system trusts another part to say. Once it's live, none of that is cheap to change.

Doing it right at the start costs very little. It's the same thinking behind my [assessment work](/services/infosec/) and [fractional CISO](/fractional-ciso/) engagements, applied while it's still cheap.
