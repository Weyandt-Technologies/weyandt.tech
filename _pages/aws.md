Cloud spending is easy to start and hard to see. Most AWS accounts I look at are paying for things no one remembers creating, and have nothing in place that would tell the owner whether the important parts are working.

Both come from the same place. No one set up visibility. It's usually cheap to fix.

{% include feature_row %}

## Why this matters past the bill

Outages and security incidents usually start the same way. Something changed and nobody noticed. The metrics that show a service slowing down are the ones that show unusual access, and the logs you'd use to debug a failure are the ones you'd need after a breach.

It's also what makes [security leadership](/fractional-ciso/) more than a title. You can't report on risk in an environment you can't see into.
